FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Finding the right SetuCrest service.

What services does SetuCrest Global provide?

SetuCrest Global operates specialist divisions for India and global sourcing, buyer and market development, and school and institutional supplies.

Can international businesses source products from India through SetuCrest?

Yes. SetuCrest Trade Solutions supports overseas and Indian businesses with supplier discovery, quotations, samples and commercial coordination in India.

How do you help Indian manufacturers find buyers?

Our Trade Solutions division researches relevant markets, importers, distributors, institutions and decision-makers, then supports focused outreach and follow-up.

Does SetuCrest supply schools and educational institutions?

Yes. SetuCrest School Supplies supports schools, colleges and institutions with educational, classroom, sports and other requirement-based supplies.

Which division should I contact?

Choose Trade Solutions for sourcing or market development and School Supplies for educational or institutional products. If your requirement overlaps, message SetuCrest Global and we will direct it appropriately.
